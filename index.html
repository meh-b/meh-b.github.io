Research

My research is in rigorous statistical mechanics. I work on Lee–Yang zeros of grand canonical partition functions, the analyticity of the infinite-volume pressure, and the uniqueness of Gibbs measures. I also work on the ground state of the Bose gas, in particular on Lieb’s Simplified Approach and the Lee–Huang–Yang formula.
