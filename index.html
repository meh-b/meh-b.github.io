Mohamed El Hedi Bahri

Hill Assistant Professor
Department of Mathematics, Rutgers University

My research is in rigorous statistical mechanics. It currently concerns the Bose gas, in particular Lieb’s Simplified Approach and the Lee–Huang–Yang formula, and Lee–Yang zeros, phase transitions and particle-number fluctuations. At Rutgers I work with Ian Jauslin and Joel L. Lebowitz.

I received my Ph.D. from Princeton University in 2023, with a thesis on thermal fluctuations of active and anisotropic elastic membranes, and my B.S. in mathematics and physics from Rutgers in 2014.

Recent papers

  • Lee–Yang zeros and particle fluctuations, with Ian Jauslin and Joel L. Lebowitz. Under review at J. Stat. Phys.
  • The Lee–Huang–Yang formula for the hard-core Simple Equation, with Ian Jauslin. Manuscript, to be submitted to Commun. Math. Phys.
