Research Interests

Rigorous statistical mechanics. The Bose gas, Lieb’s Simplified Approach and the Lee–Huang–Yang formula. Lee–Yang zeros, phase transitions and particle-number fluctuations.

Education

2023

Ph.D., Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Princeton University.

Specialization in statistical mechanics, field theory and elasticity.

Thesis Thermal fluctuations of active and anisotropic elastic membranes, adviser Andrej Košmrlj, defended February 27, 2023.

Research project with Michael Aizenman on partial differential inequalities for the random-cluster model, toward the sharpness of its phase transition. Proved the analogue of the “ϕ3” inequality of Aizenman and Barsky.

2014

B.S., Mathematics and Physics, Rutgers University, New Brunswick.

Double major, summa cum laude, with highest honors in mathematics and in physics.

Joint senior thesis in mathematics and physics, Einstein’s relativity theory, advisers Michael Kiessling and Nataša Šešum. A study of the foundations and assumptions of general relativity, with an exploration of renormalization.
