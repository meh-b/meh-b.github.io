Chained Freedom

I traversed the desert.
Coming upon no man or lesser.
East I went for farther and longer.
Until a day when the smell of water was afoot.
My mind found way, mirage after mirage.
Mirage in the rocks of the color of hay.
I drank from the source and lay in the shade.
A tree above with me in its palm.
Uneasy to rest, clocks ticked on, tock, tock.
Shifting sands like an hourglass, drop, drop.
Until time had stopped.
The smell of honey descends from above.
The dates remind me of a time of love.
A time in cages is nae the same time at all.
Before birds had ever flown.
Before the earth was sown.
And before my taste for such sweet liquor had grown.

I traversed along further.
Cursed my feet over crescents of dunes.
Till one day came the end of my straight dance.
Where a line drew between dry grass and sands.
The blades seem fuller in their guise.
In the fore, a ficus tree, I rested in its lee.
Time and its leaves passed bitterly.
As the wind did blow westwardly.
Milk dripped down from branches, drop, drop.
Until time had stopped.
The figs whisper to me of times to come.
Hiding achenes of secrets within their complexion.
Wound and wound in the same coil.
Around and around in ironed foil.
To break the pattern of induction.
The master cuffs my heart deep inside his mind.
And the clouds push on, wind-whipped without choice or liberty.
But the air is sweet of figs and draws me out like a feat of destiny.
Boundless the onwardness. Cruel the tides.

I strolled to sea and the onward blue.
A sea, I must swallow without my minds.
A sea I must shape in sips with my hands.
Beside, an apple tree with fruit born few.
But olives lay at the trunk in its lew.
Fruits did not tell of any thing they knew.
The smell of salt, inwardly, my nose drew.
Behind, an erected granite garden.
Where my old man was laid to rest and sleep.
As a giant unaware of his dreams
The lion roared just the same in his sleep.
My silence interrupted with blank stares
And his grip upon me tightened, all while
Reticent enigmas marked a stasis
Sitting, stirring, bubbling in a muddle.
Like smoke, twisted scents of salted earth reached aloft.
Showed strange attraction to the heavens, they went off.
Silhouettes of oil and sugar drifted, waft and waft.
Until time had stopped.
Seas halt their undulation, become mirrors
A shameful mirror for the balding shores.
A shadow leans over my shoulder to
Whisper with neither whisper nor senses:
‘O’ twain days thou hast traveled to this tree by two shores’
‘Twain days thou hast sat here for my arrival or more?’
‘Nae, the winds pushed me down the river to
an open mouth and turned along the coast.
If you look in the mirror or the sea
The river is East of this crescent coast
It remains virgin till the sickle shore.
I waited years on shores where stones turn to sand.’
‘Why years and not go somewhere or elsewhere?’
‘Where must I go? There is nowhere to rest.
I can only weigh the time here with grains’
‘The grains of sand here are too few to count’
‘Nae grains of sand. Grains only of words.
Words of suzerains weigh upon a stair
That rises in wisps to the firmament.
Words that—’ The moist air cuts between and the
Shadow must move on with the cloudy skies.
The clouds try to swerve, without success.
Briar roots remain in earthy fixtures.
A scent flies in on the wings of the winds:
Rose geraniums from eroded lands.
Within, stars fall to the south from lofty heights
To bathe in water springs afar from eyes.
Lands left, to sink in between other lands.
Blue seas are calling with lost sirens and I must go.

7/11

Look at me red-eyed.
Blood shot eyes
Lost in a void.
Like a bewildered
Animal. The TV
Shouts with RGB
The latest score of…
Whatever.
Seven-Up or powdery
OJ’s.
Pick up some Ruffles or crispy
Lay’s.
In and out, labels curt
But full color
Like clowns. Full of ridicule,
Now step out, use the road
Like an umbilical.

Amnesia

This land filled with angels and demons.
Away from the rise in time, they wander
For gold, for good, for blood, for spirits.
I am born into a land that is not mine.
Where clouds cast shadows longer than the mares can run

And vandals roamed with bigger sticks,
door to door
of homes of those enslaved to a single crop.
Strong arms were wet with sweat
And throats parched by sun.
Ditches dug deep in damp earth
The spade sunk sharply by his hands
Lands breathed in diurnal pulse
Brown grains sifted by her familiar hand
If grains did ever know one.
Only one woman, the tallest of three,
Rides the carousel now
With deep golden hair that strides in the wind.
She makes a honey far sweeter than figs or wine.
Beside her fields, lone trees suspend ropes.
Ropes that once bore strange fruits.
Now the children swing in fat tires that never met the road.

I must be the best.

Sweet smells across an ocean,
The plum trees in spring.
Unsilvered souls went east
to western shores
Into teeth, long and jagged.
A rush of yellow ore.
Guests of a mountain
That cuts into their minds.
Lead lodged into pores by some fiery medicine.
Immortal sooted faces lift from the iron road,
Or rise from bored soil to the setting of the sun,
Burned into memory like an edge of fading ash, alight.
And air thick with water
Stops tears in their tracks
To dry in corners and creases.
Life by the toss of a coin.
I’m in the aisle, I think of you
As a blank faceless foreigner who
Makes cheap junk.
Another coin in the till.

I must be the best.
I must work harder.
Much more than the rest.

A trickster once told me you’re a blank slate
Painted ugly, too wildly.
To kill who you are
Without killing your soul.
Turn a corner, to nurture you
In a land of angles.
The moon bore witness
To your siblings overrun
By wolves, who squeezed
The last ounces of what was free
What was yours.
Torn from love
In your land but far from home,
Your words were lost in the City of God.
Tongues pinned in writhing silence.
I see your names in parks
Full of trees and woods too young
But no trace of you.

I forgot the pain.
Round circles again.
Brothers, Sisters,
What equilibrium is this.
Now lay me to rest.

Summer

The soil broods them for seventeen years
But rather they bloom, once they have lost their fears.
Sharply rise to the music of the spheres.
Boring holes from below the ground.
They flutter, flick and flap all around.
From trees they fall and flop until the earth is found.
As they tumble and chip through the air
As though drunken, chopping through without a care.
Eyes clouded with love, find another so fair.
Gravity weighs upon them.
Till they find brown bark or stem.
On a branch, their throne, from which they perch
Cry in waves, electric chatter and chirp.
Red eyes, glazed by the dark, early stare
Across the yard of grass and thickets.
A dragonfly floats and flies, flat in ether.
A dart here, a dart there, nick the air or neither.
Double wings whip with force and command.
Such an apex, ever feared on acres of land.
Practiced predator prances from prey to prey.
While red-eyed innocence dies down under clouds of gray.
Not an ounce of grace too more or else their love will fray.
