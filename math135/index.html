Teaching

Calculus I

Slides for my Calculus I course (Math 135, 01:640:135) at Rutgers University in Fall 2026, in the order they were covered in class. Section numbers refer to the course textbook.

Lecture slides

  1. Algebra and Precalculus ReviewBrief lecture notes on the prerequisites
  2. Section 3.3: Rates of ChangeFrom average rates to the derivative
  3. Section 3.1: LimitsThe definition of a limit, and limits algebraically: back to the derivative
  4. Sections 4.1 and 4.2: The Derivative RulesBasic derivatives, constant multiples, sums and differences, products, and quotients
  5. Section 4.3: The Chain RuleDerivative of the outside, evaluated at the inside, times the derivative of the inside
  6. Sections 3.4 and 6.5: Linear ApproximationUsing the tangent line to approximate a function near a point
  7. Sections 3.2 and 3.4: ContinuityWhat it means to be continuous at a point, and how continuity relates to the derivative
  8. Section 3.1: Horizontal and Vertical AsymptotesLimits at infinity, infinite limits, and what they look like on a graph
  9. Sections 5.1 and 5.2: Increasing/Decreasing and Relative ExtremaUsing the sign of the derivative to read the shape of a graph
  10. Sections 5.1–5.3: Putting It Together — f, f′, and f″Synthesized examples: reading increase, decrease, extrema, concavity, and inflection points from formulas and from graphs
  11. Section 5.3: Concavity and the Second DerivativeHigher derivatives, inflection points, and the second derivative test
  12. Midterm ReviewKey definitions and practice problems: Sections 3.1–3.4, 4.1–4.3, 5.1–5.3, and 6.5
  13. Section 6.1: Absolute ExtremaThe largest and smallest values of a function on an interval, and the Extreme Value Theorem
  14. Section 6.2: Applications of Extrema (Part 1)Optimization: setting up and solving maximum and minimum problems
  15. Section 6.2: Applications of Extrema (Part 2)Optimization on a closed interval: a postal package and a homing pigeon
  16. Section 6.4: Related RatesHow fast one quantity changes when it is linked to another changing quantity
  17. Section 7.1: AntiderivativesUndoing the derivative, and finding a function from its rate of change
  18. Section 7.2: SubstitutionUndoing the chain rule to integrate products, quotients and compositions
  19. Section 11.1: Solutions of Elementary and Separable Differential EquationsFinding a function from an equation for its rate of change
  20. Section 11.1 (Part 2): Logistic GrowthGrowth that levels off at a carrying capacity
  21. Sections 7.3 and 7.4: Area and the Definite IntegralFrom rectangles to exact area
  22. Section 7.4: The Fundamental Theorem of CalculusDefinite integrals from antiderivatives
